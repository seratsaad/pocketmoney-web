Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026
Plain summary: we collect the minimum we need to run PocketMoney, we never sell your data, and PocketMoney is built so the people you share money with cannot see your private finances.

Who we are

PocketMoney ("we", "us") provides an AI assistant you message to manage money between people. This policy explains what we collect and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Account and early-access details you give us: your name, email, and, if you join the text-message program, your phone number.
  • Messages you send to PocketMoney and the entries you create, such as budgets, goals, and shared expenses.
  • If you connect a bank account, we access balances and transaction history through Plaid, a regulated bank-connectivity provider. Your bank credentials are entered with Plaid and your bank, never with us, and we never see them. This data is private to you and is never shown to anyone you share costs with.
  • Basic technical data such as device type, app version, and usage events (for example that the app was opened), to keep the service working and improve it. We do not record your screen and we do not capture what you type.

How your messages are processed

PocketMoney is an AI assistant, so the messages you send are processed by a third-party AI provider (OpenAI) in order to generate a reply. That means the content of your messages, including any financial details you mention, is sent to that provider to produce the response. It is used to answer you, not to train public models, and it is never used for advertising. If you would rather not have a message processed this way, do not send it.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: answer your messages, split shared costs, send reminders, and keep your records.
  • To contact you about early access and important updates.
  • To improve reliability and build new features.

The privacy wall

PocketMoney is designed so each person only sees what is meant for them. When you coordinate a shared cost, others see the status, not your income, balances, or individual spending. Private conversations and amounts stay in your own thread.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or used for their marketing.
  • We do not show your private finances to the people you share money with.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Sharing with providers

We rely on a small number of providers to run PocketMoney. They process data only on our instructions and under appropriate safeguards:

  • Plaid — bank connectivity, if you choose to connect an account.
  • OpenAI — generating the assistant's replies from your messages.
  • Stripe — subscription payments. Payments are completed on our website and card details go to Stripe, never to us.
  • Render and Turso — hosting and database.
  • Resend — sending login codes and notification emails.
  • PostHog — product analytics, limited to events such as app opens.

Your choices

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app, under Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile, groups, expenses, balances, and chat history, and disconnects any linked bank. You can also email seratmahmudsaad@gmail.com to access or correct your data and we will help.
