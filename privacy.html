Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2026
Plain summary: we collect the minimum we need to run PocketMoney, we never sell your data, and PocketMoney is built so the people you share money with cannot see your private finances.

Who we are

PocketMoney ("we", "us") provides an AI assistant you message to manage money between people. This policy explains what we collect and how we handle it.

What we collect

  • Early access details you give us: your name, email, phone number, and who PocketMoney is for.
  • Messages you send to PocketMoney and the entries you create, such as budgets, goals, and shared expenses.
  • If you choose to connect a bank account in the future, financial data is accessed through regulated providers and used only to power features you ask for.
  • Basic technical data such as device type and usage events, to keep the service working and improve it.

How we use it

  • To provide the service: answer your messages, split shared costs, send reminders, and keep your records.
  • To contact you about early access and important updates.
  • To improve reliability and build new features.

The privacy wall

PocketMoney is designed so each person only sees what is meant for them. When you coordinate a shared cost, others see the status, not your income, balances, or individual spending. Private conversations and amounts stay in your own thread.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • Mobile information and SMS opt-in consent are never shared with third parties or used for their marketing.
  • We do not show your private finances to the people you share money with.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.

Sharing with providers

We use trusted service providers to run PocketMoney, such as hosting, messaging, and, where you opt in, bank connectivity. They process data only on our instructions and under appropriate safeguards.

Your choices
